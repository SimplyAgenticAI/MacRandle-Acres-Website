Free Grok Starter Prompts

Remove Friction. Accelerate Growth.

These 10 prompts are ones we have actually used to grow a channel, write posts, follow up with clients, and fix a website. Copy one, paste it into Grok, and let it do the looking for you.

For your business

1

Audit My Website

Look at my website the way a new visitor would. List the top 5 fixes that would help more people understand what I do and take the next step, in order of impact.

2

Write This Week's Posts

Write 5 posts for this week in my voice, for the platforms I already use. Keep them short, personal, and easy to comment on. Show me each one before anything goes live.

3

Leave Real Comments

Find 5 recent posts or videos in my niche and write a genuine comment for each one, in my voice. No pitching. Show me before you post.

4

Check In With Quiet Clients

Look at who I work with and who has gone quiet. Draft a short, warm check-in for the 3 people I should reach out to first. Show me before anything is sent.

For your channel

5

New Video Ideas

What are people searching for right now in my niche? Give me 10 video ideas, starting with the best one.

6

Get My Next Video Ready

Pick the best idea for my next video. Then write me a title, a description, tags, and words to put on the thumbnail.

7

Bring Old Videos Back to Life

Find my old videos that didn't get many views but people liked. For each one, tell me the easiest way to help it: a new title and thumbnail, some Shorts cut from it, or a new edited version to upload again.

8

Make and Schedule Shorts

Pick my best long video. Find 3 good parts, turn them into Shorts with words on screen, and schedule them, one a day. Show me before they go live.

9

Get Found by New Viewers

Find 5 popular channels in my niche. Tell me what their best videos have in common and how I can make my own version that's even better.

10

Refresh My Titles and Thumbnails

Look at my channel. Rewrite the titles, descriptions, and tags on my videos so people can find them, and suggest new thumbnail wording for the ones that need it most. Show me the first batch before changing anything.

Make Your Bots Cheaper to Run

Every time a bot wakes up, checks something, or reads a long prompt, it uses up part of your plan, so trimming waste lets you do the same work for less.

1

Cut idle wake-ups

How to spot it A routine runs every 15 or 30 minutes just to see if there's work.

Fix Run it once or twice a day and do all the work in that one run.

2

Stop repeating checks

How to spot it The bot re-checks the same lists, limits, or settings every run.

Fix Check once at the start of the day, and only stop early if something goes wrong.

3

Shrink big prompts and tool calls

How to spot it Long instructions, re-reading whole files, or re-researching the same things.

Fix Keep instructions short, reuse what's already approved, and only look up what's new.

4

No chatter

How to spot it Bots sending status updates to you or each other after every step.

Fix Only message when something needs you.

Estimate your savings

An outreach bot that woke up every 30 minutes, about 140 times a week, just to send emails. Moving it to one run per weekday dropped that to 5 wake-ups a week while sending the exact same number of emails.

Simple math: count your bot's runs per week now, count them after the change, and the difference is roughly what you save on the waking-up part (the real work still costs the same).

11

Find My Bot Waste

Look at all my routines and how often each one runs. Show me which ones wake up often without doing much, repeat the same checks, or send extra status messages. For each one, suggest a cheaper schedule that gets the same results, and estimate how many runs a week I'd save. Don't change anything yet.
